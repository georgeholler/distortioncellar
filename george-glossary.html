The George Glossary

A guide to the words and phrases thrown around on the show.

  • Little Steven

    Steven Van Zandt — Musician, actor, activist, and creator of Little Steven's Underground Garage, a satellite radio station on SiriusXM channel 21.

  • LSUG, Underground Garage

    Little Steven's Underground Garage - A radio station on SiriusXM, channel 21.

  • Orphan Single

    A song released on a single that never went on an album, except on compilations.

  • Palmyra

    Palmyra Delran - DJ extraordinaire on Underground Garage, solo artist, member of The Friggs, The Coolies, and Palmyra Delran and the Doppel Gang.
    Her Trash Pop Treasures show on the Underground Garage is a great way to get introduced to garage rock, punk, power pop, and hidden underground gems.

  • Sonic Fringe

    Weekly internet radio Show on Luxuria.com, hosted by Moana Santana.

  • Wicked Cool Records

    Record label created by Steven Van Zandt that champions new rock & roll, garage rock, power pop, and roots rock.
