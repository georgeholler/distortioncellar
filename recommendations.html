  • Radio
    George has listened to Little Steven's Underground Garage (LSUG) on SiriusXM channel 21 since 2015.
    What distinguishes this station:
    • The variety of music
    • The DJs are highly knowledgeable — they are either musicians, famous DJs, or musical scholars
    • They give info about the songs, who wrote them, when they were released, who produced them, and so much more. They also tell stories that are highly entertaining.
  • LSUG DJ Shows

    Honestly, every DJ and every show is amazing.
    Do yourself a favor and try them out.

  • Internet Radio Show

    Sonic Fringe on Luxuria.com
    The show is curated and hosted by Moana Santana.
    It is a live show from 6–8 p.m. (Eastern) on Tuesdays.
    Moana spins a fresh, highly eclectic variety of artists and tracks.
    You cannot listen to her show without going "Wow" many times.
