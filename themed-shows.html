Themed Shows

Special episodes built around a single idea, era, or genre.

  • Mixed Bag

    Not really a themed show, just loosely connected song sets.

  • Planets

    A Planets episode will have song blocks where the songs match a planet's two-word description.
    For example: Mercury is Frenetic and Fiery, so you can expect songs that are fast-paced and loud.


    The Planets
    • Mercury — Frenetic, Fiery
    • Venus — Seductive, Romantic
    • Earth — Rustic, Organic
    • Mars — Angry, Defiant
    • Jupiter — Massive, Powerful
    • Saturn — Cosmic, Hypnotic
    • Uranus — Raunchy, Immature
    • Neptune — Dark, Brooding
    • Pluto — Stark, Distant

    Considered Heavenly Bodies:
    • The Sun — Radiant, Uplifting
    • The Moon — Wondrous, Reflective
  • Years

    Songs from a specific year.
    The shows will focus on the significance of the songs in that year and how they changed the musical landscape.

  • Decade Time Machine

    Songs from a specific decade.
    The shows will take the listener on a journey through time to enjoy music.
    Shows will focus on a genre or will be multi-genre.

  • Songwriters

    Shows focused on a songwriter or songwriting team.

  • Covers

    Shows built around covers of an artist's songs.

  • Events/Venues

    Shows focused on special events like festivals or famous venues.
